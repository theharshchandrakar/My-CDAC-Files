foreign key
Referential Integrity: Allowing NULL values in a foreign key column does not violate referential integrity.

	A foreign key constraint requires that any non-NULL value must 	match a primary or unique key in the parent table.
	
	Since NULL means "unknown" or "no value," it is treated as the 	absence of a relationship and is ignored by the referential 	integrity check.

Outliers
Extreme values in the data set which are away from normal values by a abnormal distance.

An outlier in statistics is a data point that differs significantly from the overall pattern of a dataset. These extreme values can be much higher or much lower than the other observations and may have a large impact on statistical analyses.

median
	odd n
		(n+1)/2

mean median are same in perfectly distributed data (rarely happens in real life)

mode- most frequent value

median (second quartile)- divides the data in 2 equal parts, 50% above 50% below.
1st quartile 25 % below 75% above
3rd quartile 70% below 25% above

IQR - InterQuartileRange (diff between Q3-Q1)

Upper limit = Q3 + (1.5 * IQR)
Upper Whisker

Lower limit = Q1 - (1.5 * IQR)
