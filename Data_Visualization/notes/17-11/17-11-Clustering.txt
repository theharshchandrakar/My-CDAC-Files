5. Moving Average
Line Smoothening technique - reduces short-term changes in the data
A moving average in time series analysis is a calculation used to smooth out short-term fluctuations and highlight longer-term trends by creating a series of averages of different subsets of the data. 
Default moving average in Tableau is 3 period moving average (average of current & 2 previous values)
