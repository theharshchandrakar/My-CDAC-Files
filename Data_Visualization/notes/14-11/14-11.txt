Line Chart – used to represent data of time series

To create line chart, use one date field and one measure. Always add the date field in column and measure in  row for line chart.

Two varieties of line chart-
1. Discrete - Here the line chart is created for individual date part e.g. day, month, quarter, etc.
	Shown in BLUE color.
2. Continuous - Here the line chart is created using year and individual date part month or quarter.
	Shown in GREEN color
