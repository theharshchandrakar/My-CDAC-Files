Measure - Any numeric column (with or without decimal) on which maths or stats operations can be performed to generate meaningful result in business.
	Mean and Median common stats operation.
	e.g. Profit, tax, discount, quantity, cost, expense, etc.

Dimension - Any numeric or character or data column which is not a measure and can be used to aggregate measures.
	e.g. department, name, address, city, categories, DOB, joining date, rating, etc.
	
	Any primary or foreign key column is always considered as Dimension.
	Only numeric dimension allowed on DIMENSION is COUNT.
